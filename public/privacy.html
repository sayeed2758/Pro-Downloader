Privacy Policy

This template should be reviewed and adapted to your actual operator, jurisdiction, services and data flows before public release.

Information We May Collect

Account information such as name and email when you sign in; download/process history; saved bookmarks, favorite sites, watched entries and basic device/browser information needed to operate the service.

Firebase

Firebase Authentication and Realtime Database may process account and application data according to your configuration and Firebase's terms and privacy documentation.

Third-Party APIs

When enabled, a resolver or payment provider may receive the link or transaction data necessary to provide its service. Configure only providers you are authorized to use.

Payments

Payment information should be handled by the selected payment provider. Pro Downloader should not claim to store card information unless it actually does.

Cookies and Local Storage

The web app may use localStorage for themes, guest history, tabs and settings.

Data Retention

Define retention periods appropriate to your deployment. Users should be given a clear account deletion path.

Security

Never send passwords, authentication tokens or server secrets to client logs. The backend uses URL validation, SSRF protection, CORS controls, authentication checks, timeouts and rate limiting.

Children's Privacy

Do not intentionally target children with unrestricted media downloading. Apply the legal requirements that govern your deployment.

External Links

Third-party websites are governed by their own policies.

Contact

Use the support address configured in Admin and the Telegram support channel.
